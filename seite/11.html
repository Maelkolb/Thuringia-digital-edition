Fußnote: alle Höhen in preußischen Dezimalfuß, auf den Pegel bei Swinemünde bezogen.Mountain names on the right (Bramthal, little Schnauder, Aga) and left bank of the Elster (five sections); altitude of the inhabited places in the Unterland (Caaschwitz lowest, Käseschenke highest, difference 493′; distribution over altitude classes) and start of the altitude list for the right bank of the Elster. Footnote: all altitudes in Prussian decimal feet, referred to the Swinemünde gauge.
